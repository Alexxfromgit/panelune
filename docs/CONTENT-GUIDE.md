# Content guide and future store release

Reviewed against official platform documentation on **5 October 2026**. This is an editorial implementation guide, not a claim of store approval.

## Boundaries for this collection

The intended audience is adults aged 18 and older. All named principal characters are aged 27–41. The first edition uses romance, emotional conflict, grief, fantasy, and atmospheric suspense. No published chapter includes nudity, sex acts, erotic poses, explicit dialogue, sexual violence, graphic injuries, weapons, drugs, gambling, or user-generated material. Each story exposes its content notes and adult character ages before the reader opens.

Romance is conveyed through companionship, glances, dialogue, and emotional stakes. Artwork is fully clothed. These boundaries apply to covers, promotional assets, reader pages, dialogue, and future episodes alike. The reference websites informed the episode-reader concept only; their characters, scripts, and artwork were not reused.

## Apple

[App Review Guidelines](https://developer.apple.com/app-store/review/guidelines/), especially 1.1.4, prohibit overtly sexual or pornographic material. Section 2.3.6 requires honest age-rating answers; 2.3.8 also restricts store metadata and promotional imagery to general-audience presentation. An adult classification does not waive those rules.

[Age ratings values and definitions](https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/age-ratings-values-and-definitions/) explains the current categories and content descriptors. Complete the actual questionnaire against the final app and entire catalog. Apple’s generated rating and any allowed override must be determined in App Store Connect; the website’s 18+ marker is not that rating.

## Google Play

[Inappropriate Content](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9878810?hl=en) restricts pornography, sexually gratifying material, and other prohibited sexual content. Its limited catalog provisions do not create a general permission for explicit comics. Panelune’s initial catalog does not depend on an exception.

[Content Ratings](https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9898843?hl=en) requires a completed rating questionnaire and uses IARC classifications, which can differ by territory. The adult target-audience selection and the calculated content rating are separate declarations. Reassess them when adding episodes or functionality.

## Release considerations

Before a mobile submission, review the final catalog and marketing materials against the policies in effect then. Complete store questionnaires accurately, confirm applicable territory-specific age controls, and review the app’s actual privacy and functionality. The static website’s label and content-note button do not verify age or protect access to restricted content. No restricted content is included in this edition.

The current static build stores a reading list, progress, and one style preference locally. If accounts, analytics, payments, comments, uploads, or recommendations are added, their associated disclosures and platform requirements must be addressed as part of that feature. Native apps and store submission have not been built or reviewed in this project.
